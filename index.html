Laser-Based Lid Inspection System

Non-contact laser triangulation measures lid deformation to identify potentially faulty closures. A pneumatic pusher diverts flagged jars to a reject collection area. Side guide rails help prevent tipping while a line operator monitors system performance.

Core Design Objectives

Safety — — Reduce the risk of faulty seals reaching downstream operations. Mitigate laser exposure, collisions and tipping. Not every leak defect can necessarily be detected.

Profitable — — Balance throughput, equipment cost, false rejects, operator oversight and maintenance.

Safety and Feasibility Assumptions

The link between lid displacement and seal integrity needs experimental validation using passing and defective jars. Pusher response, consecutive rejects, guide-rail openings and operator protection must be evaluated on site.

End-to-End Inspection Process

01 · FeedCapped jars enter the guarded conveyor
02 · MeasureLaser triangulation measures lid displacement
03 · DecideController makes a pass/fail decision
04 · SortFailed jars are diverted by the pneumatic pusher; passing jars continue
05 · SuperviseOperator monitors the pusher, conveyor and reject area
